Citing edgeofpy
===============

If you use edgeofpy, please cite it. GitHub's "Cite this repository" button
on the `repository page <https://github.com/jnobyrne/edgeofpy>`_ gives the
citation in APA and BibTeX formats, generated from the ``CITATION.cff`` file.

Please also cite the original papers of the methods you use; they are listed
in the docstrings of the functions in the :doc:`api`.
